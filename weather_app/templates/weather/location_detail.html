About Historical Climate Analysis

Choose a month and day, then run the analysis. Each result uses a centered 7-day weather average for that calendar day across completed years only, so every sample includes the full week.

Unusual weeks are defined from that historical distribution:

  • Warm: warmest 10%
  • Cool: coolest 10%
  • Wet: wettest 10%
  • Dry: driest 10%

NOAA climate indexes describe broad climate patterns. A correlation near +1 or -1 is stronger than one near 0, but it shows association, not causation.
